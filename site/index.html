X for macOSUnofficial

Your X feeds,
in their own window.

A Mac app that keeps X's For you and Following feeds open in a dedicated window. It refreshes the feed every minute, and waits while you read, write, or watch.

Download No release yet

X for macOS is an independent app. It is not made, endorsed, or supported by X Corp. It shows the x.com website, and you sign in to X yourself.

What it does

Refreshes, then waits

Reloads the home feed every 60 seconds. It waits while you are scrolled down, typing a post, watching a video, or using a menu.

Following shows Recent

Picks Recent each time Following opens. You can turn this off in the View menu.

Pull to refresh

Pull down at the top of the feed with a trackpad or mouse wheel to refresh now.

Mac controls

Back, Forward, Home, find on page, text size, a Dock badge for notifications, and keyboard shortcuts for both feeds.

Protects drafts

Asks before a reload, navigation, or quit would discard a post you haven't sent.

Collects nothing

No account of its own, no telemetry, and no saved posts. Links to other sites open in your browser.
